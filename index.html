Kolay Fener Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Information collection

Kolay Fener does not collect your name, email address, location, photos, videos, or other personal data. The current version contains no account creation, analytics service, advertising SDK, or tracking technology.

Camera and vibration permissions

Camera permission is used only to turn the device flashlight on and off. The app does not take photos or videos, record camera output, or send any content off the device. Vibration permission is used for button feedback only when enabled by the user.

On-device settings

Language, vibration, SOS, and screen-light preferences are stored only on your device and are not sent to BB Arts or any other server.

Internet, advertising, and third parties

Kolay Fener works without an internet connection. The current version displays no advertising and uses no third-party service that shares data. If advertising or an online feature is added, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children's privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. Privacy questions: aydoganbusra262@gmail.com
